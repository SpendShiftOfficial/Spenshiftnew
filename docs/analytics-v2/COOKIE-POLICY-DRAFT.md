## Section 4 — Replace analytics and advertising measurement

Basic statistics do not require optional advertising or analytics cookies. We use limited event types and coarse categories, aggregate them by day and do not create a visitor profile. New basic measurement starts only under the revised notice and is disabled if your preferences cannot be read. You may turn it off separately in Privacy settings. We honour earlier choices: basic statistics remain off for a browser with a prior preference unless you expressly enable them under the revised notice. Optional choices do not override a basic-statistics opt-out. No raw financial answers or private links are sent in the new measurement requests.

Optional website analytics and advertising are separate. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load only when permitted for optional website analytics on reviewed public pages. Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel load only when permitted for optional advertising on reviewed public pages. Optional scripts do not run on private audit, results, paid questionnaire or report pages. Existing consented first-party audit and report activity counters continue only when applicable optional analytics permission exists. We do not forward rejected or unset optional choices' activity to advertising tools through cookieless pings or server-side tracking.

We use versioned browser storage to remember privacy preferences; it contains choices and a policy version, not a visitor identifier. When a new choice restricts previously permitted optional categories, we also make the old all-or-nothing preference conservative so reverting software does not re-enable a withdrawn category. The new basic-statistics choice is independent. We retain basic and previously consented aggregate totals and the separate aggregate reporting cache for up to 180 days. Separate hosting/security logs may process technical information such as IP addresses.

## Section 6 — Replace your choices

Use Privacy settings, available from the footer and eligible product pages, to accept or reject optional website analytics and advertising, manage the two categories separately, or disable basic statistics. For an older consent choice, you may voluntarily enable the new basic statistics after reading the revised notice. Changing optional settings does not change your separate basic-statistics choice. When you withdraw optional permission we stop loading the relevant scripts, refresh the document where needed, and attempt to expire known first-party provider cookies. Other cookies and previously transmitted information may be controlled by your browser or the provider. You may change your browser settings or use provider opt-out tools where available. Disabling optional tracking or basic statistics does not remove access to SpendShift; necessary storage or payment services may be needed for particular features.
