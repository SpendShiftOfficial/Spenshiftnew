# Privacy impact review — release decision sheet

**25 November 2026 | No legal sign-off or authority to activate.**

## Scope and legal basis

Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) compilation effective 4 June 2026; APP 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 11 as applicable. Whether SpendShift is an APP entity (turnover, related bodies, trading information for a benefit and other exceptions) remains a solicitor determination. Apply APP-standard controls regardless. Australian Consumer Law s 18 means the current promise that rejection disables first-party counts cannot silently be reversed. OAIC guidance stresses fair collection, transparency, reasonable expectations, de-identification risk and overseas disclosures. An aggregate final table does not establish that all in-transit data is anonymous.

## Approved design versus excluded collection

Proposed basic first-party events: public arrivals/page views, finite source/campaign/device/browser categories, visible engagement thresholds, scroll, approved clicks and FAQs, Web Vitals ranges and safe errors, audit progression, valid results and offer/checkout observations. No person-level journey, tracking identifiers or financial answers. Store each dimension as a separate marginal count. Do not publish or infer exact unique visitors, linked conversion, actual savings or campaign purchase attribution. Direct/unknown referrer and reloads/fast exits/bots limit accuracy.

Payment/fulfilment aggregates use existing necessary operational records under a reviewed reporting purpose. Stripe confirmed amounts/refunds are distinct from order status and must be reconciled separately; source unavailable is not zero. Owner-entered aggregate Stripe/Meta/GA4 daily totals are an explicit manual reporting cache, not automated or independently verified provider feeds. Only complete covered periods return a source total. Source/date replacement is idempotent; explicit zero must be verified. No third-party browser tracking anywhere in the candidate. No expanded answer statistics, detailed geography, unique IDs, optional-choice counting, private paid-stage or private report tracking without separate approval. The separately requested three-bucket market estimate on public arrival events is included only for legal review; not approved for collection. Existing consented audit/report counts retain original scope.

## 27 November 2026 — consent compliance correction (draft, pending independent approval)

The three legal-review findings are addressed in the isolated code, subject to final tests and counsel review. The unchanged event catalogue, three-bucket audience marginal, 180-day aggregate retention, paid-report consent scope and owner authentication remain in place. No new analytics field, identifier, external service, vendor tracker or production resource was added.

**Staged release:** The fallback and granular disabled modes save basicOff=true for any saved choice; later activation leaves it off until an explicit basic-enable action after the active eligible notice has been presented. A first optional choice saved before notice presentation also defaults basic off. Existing legacy consent and independently selected basic opt-outs remain protected. No inference of consent from silence.

**Notice:** the concise in-flow disclosure and immediate three actions (accept optional, reject optional, all statistics off) must be in the visible viewport across two animation frames before v2 events. Hidden, offscreen, occluded or prerendered pages do not satisfy the initial gate. The gate remains valid for the document on eligible pages after presentation so scrolling and product navigation remain useful. Policy links and independent basic controls are accessible in the one-step settings panel. Visibility is not proof of comprehension or consent. Confirm actual browser, accessibility and legally sufficient APP 5 notice; a notice gate is not a guarantee that all infrastructure-level IP/log processing begins only after disclosure.

**Withdrawal:** shared document-wide fail-closed guard covers v2 and original optional audit/report sends. Validate the stored schema, reject malformed values, verify writes (including conservative legacy downgrade) by read-back, and show failure instead of success. Failed saves stop both categories in the affected document, with a best-effort non-identifying per-tab sessionStorage marker surviving reload where it can be stored. Retry verified saving to persist choices; optional choice after failure keeps basic off unless explicitly enabled under the active notice. If both storage mechanisms fail, the in-memory guard stops new sends in the current document, but it cannot ensure other tabs, later documents or devices honour an unsaved choice. Already dispatched requests and aggregate counts are not retroactively undone. An explicit all-off action may repair a malformed stored choice without touching the audit draft. Successful persisted choices propagate through storage on the same origin where supported.
